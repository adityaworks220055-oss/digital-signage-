How the queue works

  1. Each server group has its own queue of up to {{max_queue_items}} image groups.
  2. An image group can only be queued when its image count matches the number of servers in the group.
  3. Play next plays item 1 and removes it from the queue. Play now plays a group immediately and leaves the queue unchanged.
  4. Use the arrows to reorder items, or ✕ to remove one. Renaming or deleting an image group updates the queues automatically.
